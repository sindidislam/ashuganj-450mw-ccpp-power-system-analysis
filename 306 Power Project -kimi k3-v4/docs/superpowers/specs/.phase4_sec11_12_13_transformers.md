### 13.4 Tertiary-grounding effect on LG/LLG (binding — direction stated)

1. The tertiary is a CLOSED DELTA (d11) with NO neutral, NO earth connection, NO
   external terminals, NO load (rows 8, 11; `topology_validation.md:16`). "Tertiary
   grounding" therefore means exactly one thing: the delta LOOP is a closed
   zero-sequence circulating path (zero voltage induced round the delta drives a
   circulating current limited by the tertiary-branch leakage `Z_T`), NOT an
   earthed-neutral path. There is no tertiary `ZN` to specify — any test that inserts
   one is a modelling error.
2. Effect: the closed delta provides ampere-turn balance for HV/LV zero currents and
   LOWERS the zero impedance seen from HV and LV relative to the tertiary-open case.
   CONSEQUENCE (review-critical): the §13.3 primary (tertiary OPEN/omitted) OVERSTATES
   the GAT zero impedance and therefore UNDERSTATES GAT-fed LG/LLG currents on the
   230-kV and 6.9-kV sides. The omission is NON-CONSERVATIVE for current magnitude
   (conservative for retained voltage) — this is WHY the §13.6 bounding sensitivities
   are MANDATORY, not optional, and why §13.7 forbids silent reuse of the Phase-3
   abstraction.
3. LL/LLL note: the unloaded delta carries no positive- or negative-sequence current
   in any balanced or unbalanced fundamental-frequency fault network (no external
   terminals, no source) — tertiary affects LG/LLG (zero-involved) types ONLY. Task 12
   SHALL gate the tertiary-sensitivity legs to LG/LLG reporting (LLL/LL runs are
   identical across H-legs by construction — a required Task 12 consistency check).
