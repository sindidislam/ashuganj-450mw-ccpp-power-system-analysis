### 14.2 Physical reasoning (why the band sits where it sits)

1. Earth-return NECESSITY: positive-sequence currents sum to zero (no earth
   participation); zero-sequence currents are co-phasal (`3·I0` in earth/ground
   wires). The zero loop therefore ALWAYS encloses a larger flux area (conductor→
   earth→conductor) and ALWAYS includes earth-path resistance. Hence for an
   OVERHEAD ACSR link: `R0 > R1`, `X0 > X1`, `B0 < B1` — directionally certain
   from Maxwell/earth-return physics (Carson form), magnitude-bounded by §14.3.
   Any assumption with `R0 ≤ R1`, `X0 ≤ X1`, or `B0 ≥ B1` is REJECTED as
   unphysical for this plant (§14.7).
2. What pushes each ratio UP vs DOWN (bounds rationale, not a computation):
   ground (earth) wires in parallel with earth LOWER R0 and X0 (shielding +
   parallel return) and LOWER B0 (electrostatic shielding); high soil resistivity,
   absent/ineffective ground wires, wide phase spacing, and untransposed short
   construction RAISE R0 and X0; tight GIS-adjacent spacings and double-circuit
   proximity add mutual terms that RAISE the effective lumped zero (§14.4).
   Because E6 leaves EVERY one of these unknowns open, the band SHALL span the
   shielded/low-soil end to the unshielded/high-soil end — narrowing it would
   require the missing geometry.
3. Short-line SCALE argument (why a wide band is practical): at 0.7 km the
   largest band-end absolute (`X0_eq = 3.5·X_eq ≈ 3.5·0.14 ≈ 0.50 ohm`, eq. 14-2
   form) remains an order below the adjacent transformer/grid zero legs (GSUT
   `15.8 %`/515 MVA ≈ tens-of-ohms at 230 kV; grid `|Z| ≈ 2.66–2.95 ohm). The
   line-zero band therefore moves HV LG/LLG results as a bounded perturbation,
   not a regime change — wide-but-honest bounds are BOTH technically defensible
   AND study-practical. The implementation SHALL report the band-end absolute
   spread as a diagnostic (§14.6-S1).
4. Capacitance DIRECTION: earth image + ground-wire shielding reduce the zero
   capacitance below the positive value for overhead construction (`B0/B1 < 1`);
   `B0 = B1` would deny the earth plane, `B0 > B1` belongs to cable/sheath
   physics (not this plant). The §14.3 `kB` band stays strictly below 1.0; its
