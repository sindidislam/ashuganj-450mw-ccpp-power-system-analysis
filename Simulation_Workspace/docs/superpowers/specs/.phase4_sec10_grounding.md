## 10.2 Impedance-reflection equations (design, symbolic — no final numerics)

The NER transformer is treated as an IDEAL single-phase ratio for reflection
purposes; its own series impedance is handled separately (§10.3.3). All equations
are stated in PHYSICAL ohms at the stated side; pu conversion is §10.5.

```text
(10-1)  Turns ratio (single-phase, neutral-to-ground reference)
        n  =  V1 / V2,   V1 = 22/sqrt(3) kV,   V2 = 500 V
        NOTE: V1 is ALREADY phase-to-ground. No further sqrt(3) division
        applies inside the reflection — the sqrt(3) lives in the rating only.

(10-2)  Secondary-to-primary reflection (general form)
        Z_HV  =  n^2 · Z_LV
        where Z_LV is any impedance across the 500-V secondary and Z_HV is
        its equivalent seen from the generator neutral terminal.

(10-3)  Loading-resistor transfer (resistive special case of 10-2)
        R_refl  =  n^2 · R_loading,   R_loading = 2.62 ohm (SOURCE/PRIMARY, §10.1 row 7)
        R_refl is the DESIGN transfer of the physical resistor to the HV side.
        Its reconciliation with the QUOTED figure R_HV_quoted ≈ 60 ohm
        (SOURCE/PRIMARY, §10.1 row 6) is MANDATORY per §10.3.4 — the two rows
        are separate source figures and SHALL NOT be silently merged.
```

## 10.3 Equivalent neutral impedance ZN seen from the generator neutral

### 10.3.1 Definition

```text
(10-4)  ZN  =  R_NER_HV  +  Z_NGT_series_HV
        where R_NER_HV  = HV-side resistive equivalent from the 10BAB11
                          arrangement (§10.2 transfer + §10.3.4 reconciliation),
              Z_NGT_series_HV = NER transformer series impedance referred to HV
                          (MISSING per §10.1 row 8; rule §10.3.3).
        ZN is a PHASE-NEUTRAL ohmic quantity at the 22-kV level, seen looking
        from the generator neutral terminal toward earth through the NER path.
```

### 10.3.2 Resistive dominance (design intent, not a computed result)

The arrangement is HIGH-RESISTANCE grounding by construction (NGT + loading
resistor). The design intent is: Re(ZN) dominates Im(ZN) by at least an order of
magnitude after the §10.3.3 treatment is applied. The implementation SHALL report
the R/X split of the adopted ZN and SHALL NOT inject an un-sourced reactance to
"tune" any fault level.

### 10.3.3 MISSING series-impedance rule (Z_NGT_series_HV)

Because the NER transformer series impedance and magnetising branch are MISSING
(§10.1 row 8), the implementation SHALL adopt exactly one of the following,
stated explicitly:

- (a) NEGLECT with justification: set `Z_NGT_series_HV = 0`, justified in text by
  resistor dominance (the reflected loading resistance is the current-limiting
  element); or
- (b) BOUNDED assumption: adopt a stated uk%-style bound with
  ENGINEERING_ASSUMPTION label, physical justification, and mandatory sensitivity
  (Task 13 case E).

In NEITHER case may the adopted value carry SOURCE/PRIMARY status. The magnetising
branch is neglected in both variants (open-circuit approximation behind the
loaded secondary), stated — never hidden.

### 10.3.4 Mandatory reconciliation (quoted ~60 ohm vs reflected 2.62 ohm)

The implementation SHALL evaluate eq. (10-3) and reconcile the result against
`R_HV_quoted ≈ 60 ohm` (§10.1 row 6), and SHALL record exactly one outcome:

- (i) AGREE within stated tolerance → adopt the reconciled resistive value, cite
  both source rows, record the tolerance; or
- (ii) DISAGREE → keep BOTH figures visible, adopt one as primary with written
  physical rationale (e.g. quoted figure includes transformer/lead effects, or the
  reflection is the governing physics), carry the other as the Task 13-E
  sensitivity alternative. DISAGREEMENT SHALL NOT be resolved by editing either
  source value.

### 10.3.5 3×ZN zero-sequence treatment

```text
(10-5)  In the ZERO-sequence network ONLY, the neutral impedance appears as
        3·ZN in series in the generator neutral branch (§10.4.1).
        The factor 3 arises because the neutral carries 3·I0 while the
        zero-sequence network is per-phase:  V_N = ZN·(3·I0)  ⟺  V0_drop = (3·ZN)·I0.
        The 3× factor SHALL NEVER appear in the positive- or negative-sequence
        networks, and ZN SHALL NEVER be inserted without it in the zero-sequence
        network (no "1×ZN in zero-seq" variant except as a labelled Task 13-E
        error-check sensitivity, if Task 13 admits it).
```

## 10.4 Zero-sequence entry — exactly where the NER inserts

### 10.4.1 Insertion point (binding)

The NER impedance inserts EXCLUSIVELY in the GENERATOR NEUTRAL BRANCH of the
zero-sequence network:

- Topology: generator zero-sequence internal branch `jX0_gen` (value/classification
  owned by Task 9 — NOT ruled here) runs from the generator zero-sequence terminal
  to the generator neutral node N; from node N, the impedance `3·ZN` (eq. 10-5)
  runs from N to the EARTH REFERENCE node.
- Current: the N–earth leg carries `3·I0_gen` (the generator's zero-sequence
  neutral current). Positive- and negative-sequence neutral currents do not exist
  in this branch by construction.
- Fault-type gating (hook to Task 12 fault mathematics): the N–earth leg is
  ENERGISED for earth-fault types LG and LLG (zero-sequence network excited, Zf
  inserted per Task 12). It is DEAD (carries no current, may be omitted or shown
  open) for LLL and bolted LL faults, which excite no zero-sequence network.

### 10.4.2 Q5-part statement (generator-neutral leg of the zero path)

Generator earth-fault current on the 22-kV side is limited PRINCIPALLY by `3·ZN`
in series with `jX0_gen`: the NER is the dominant zero-sequence element for LG
faults at the generator terminals / GSUT LV interface. Conversely, the generator
NER participates in NO 230-kV-side, South-line, or remote-bus earth fault: the
GSUT YNd1 LV delta blocks generator zero-sequence transfer to the HV side
(detail owned by Task 8 — this section asserts the BLOCKING CONSEQUENCE only).
UAT/GAT LV zero-sequence paths are owned by Task 8; line/grid zero legs by
Tasks 9/10. Full zero-path assembly (review-gate Q5 in full) is Task 9's.

### 10.4.3 What is NOT in this branch

Auxiliary-system grounding (UAT LV 5-A limit), GAT tertiary grounding, station/
GIS earthing grids, cable-sheath paths, and surge-arrester paths are NOT part of
the generator neutral branch and SHALL NOT be lumped into ZN. Any such path
enters only through its owning task's section (Task 8/9) with its own provenance.

## 10.5 Sign and base conventions (binding)

1. SIGN: passive-sign convention throughout. ZN is positive-real dominant
   (resistive); voltage drop is taken in the direction neutral-node-N → earth,
   i.e. `V_N0 − V_earth = (3·ZN)·I0_gen` with `V_earth ≡ 0`. No negative-resistance
   or active-source representation of the NER is permitted.
2. OHMIC REFERENCE: ZN in ohms is referenced to generator-neutral phase-to-ground
   quantities at the 22-kV level (base voltage for pu conversion is the PHASE
   voltage consistently with the stated base power — see rule 3).
3. PU CONVERSIONS (equation form; implementation evaluates):
   `ZN_pu_machine = ZN_ohm / Zbase_machine`, `Zbase_machine = 22²/458 ohm`
   (frozen Task 4 convention, QUALIFIED interpretation, §6.3);
   study-base transfer `ZN_pu_study = ZN_pu_machine · (S_study/S_machine)` at the
   same voltage level, with any cross-voltage-level treatment stated explicitly
   per Task 4 §6.3 rule 3. The `3×` factor (eq. 10-5) applies AFTER pu conversion
   identically (linear scaling) — order SHALL be stated, result is identical.
4. PER-UNIT vs OHMISC reporting: the implementation SHALL report ZN in BOTH ohms
   (at neutral) and pu (machine base + study base) with the base printed beside
   every figure. A bare "ZN =" with no base is FORBIDDEN.
5. SYMMETRICAL-COMPONENT FRAME: `I0 = (Ia + Ib + Ic)/3`, neutral current
   `IN = 3·I0`; the `3·ZN` leg satisfies KCL at node N against the generator
   zero-sequence terminal current. Task 12 owns the LG/LLG sequence-connection
   mathematics that consumes this branch.
